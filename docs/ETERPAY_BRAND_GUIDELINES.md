# EterPay — Brand Identity Guidelines

> Tagline: "Your Money. Everywhere. Intelligently Connected."

---

## 1. Symbol & Geometry
The EterPay mark represents perpetual liquidity, global interconnectivity, and deterministic security:
- **Concentric Payment Orbits:** Two mathematical concentric circles symbolizing the local settlement loop and global cross-border rail.
- **Directional Velocity Arc:** Tangent trajectory vector with terminal node representing precision payout delivery.
- **Colorway:** Forest Green (`#0f4d40`) core on Cream (`#fbf9f5`), with inverted variant (`#fbf9f5` on `#111111`) for dark surfaces.

---

## 2. Asset Manifest (`/public/brand/`)
- `logo-horizontal.svg`: Primary horizontal lockup combining the EterPay concentric mark with the editorial serif wordmark.
- `logo-dark.svg`: High-contrast ivory lockup on charcoal for footer, terminal, and mobile headers.
- `logo-icon.svg`: Standalone 48x48 icon mark for favicons, browser tabs, and app store listings.
- `favicon.svg`: 32x32 rounded rectangular badge for mobile bookmarks and web manifest.

---

## 3. Typography Floor
- **Display Headings:** `ivypresto-headline`, with web-safe fallback `Playfair Display`. Weights: 400 (regular) and 500 (medium).
- **Body & Controls:** `neue-haas-unica`, with web-safe fallback `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Weights: 400 (regular), 500 (medium), and 600 (semibold).
- **Numbers & Monetary Figures:** `tabular-nums` enforced across all account ledgers, prices, and conversion ratios.
