# Global Payment Interoperability Roadmap — EterPay Anywhere

> **Phased Evolution from platform deployment Prototype to Global Universal Payment Engine**

---

## Stage 1 — platform deployment Prototype (Current Milestone)
* **Goal:** Deliver a working, end-to-end payment demonstration of global routing.
* **Integrations:**
  * **PayPal Orders v2 Sandbox:** Fully operational live integration with server-side creation, modal approval, capture, and webhook logging.
  * **Frankfurter ECB Rates:** Live interbank reference feed with 1-hour in-memory cache.
  * **Payment Orchestration Engine:** Multi-provider routing between PayPal Sandbox, Simulated UPI International, and Simulated Cards.
  * **Universal QR Parser:** Demonstrating parsing of EMVCo Merchant QRs and UPI URI strings.
  * **AG Grid Ledger:** Unified multi-currency transaction audit trail with AI auto-filter.
  * **Status:** **Delivered & Verified ✅**.

---

## Stage 2 — Initial Authorized Integrations (Post-platform deployment)
* **Goal:** Expand real payment rails beyond PayPal sandbox.
* **Milestones:**
  * Connect certified card payment processors (Stripe / Adyen / CyberSource).
  * Direct merchant QR generation for EterPay Business accounts.
  * Partner with licensed Remittance Service Providers (RSPs) for select cross-border corridors.

---

## Stage 3 — Regional Interoperability Alliances
* **Goal:** Enable bilateral domestic payment rail interoperability.
* **Milestones:**
  * Partner with certified Indian Bank PSPs for formal NIPL UPI International linkage.
  * Aggregate Asian regional digital wallets via Alipay+ developer program.
  * Support Pix QR payments for Brazilian inbound/outbound travel corridors.

---

## Stage 4 — Global Connected Financial Operating System
* **Goal:** Seamless local-like payment experiences in 50+ countries.
* **Milestones:**
  * Intelligent multi-rail dynamic cost routing (least-cost routing between card, bank rail, and wallet).
  * Instant multi-currency virtual accounts via regulated Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) partners.
  * Full autonomous compliance and real-time AML/sanctions screening engine.
