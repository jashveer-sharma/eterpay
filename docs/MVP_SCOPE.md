# EterPay — Hackathon MVP Scope (P0 Capabilities)

> **Verified, Working Minimum Viable Product for Hackathon Judging**

---

## 1. Delivered & Verified Capabilities (P0)

1. **Authentication & Shell:** Full-stack architecture with Next.js 15, FastAPI, and Hallmark Dusk design system.
2. **Deterministic Payment Gate:** Real server-side PayPal Orders v2 `create-order` and `capture-order` with UUIDv4 idempotency and human authorization card.
3. **Modal Checkout:** Embedded `@paypal/react-paypal-js` button supporting sandbox buyer testing.
4. **Live European Central Bank FX:** Real-time rates from Frankfurter API with 1-hour in-memory cache and transparent fee breakdown.
5. **Interactive Currency Calculator:** Instant reactive converter across 30+ fiat currencies.
6. **Multi-Tier AI Copilot:** Gemini 1.5 Flash + Groq Llama 3.3 70B fallback + deterministic rule engine with structured tool calling and conversational memory.
7. **Multi-Destination Travel Budgeting:** Automated breakdown for Tokyo, Paris, London, Dubai, Singapore, Bali, New York, Bangkok.
8. **AG Grid Financial Terminal:** Community v32 transaction ledger with custom cell renderers, sorting, pagination, and dynamic AI natural language filtering.
9. **Agentic Commerce:** Channel3 product search and local travel catalog with one-click checkout cart preparation.
10. **Macro FX Stress-Testing Simulator:** Interactive shock slider with real-time portfolio impact calculations and permanent simulation badges.
